# Content contract

A scenario records:

- learning objective and learner level;
- communicative context and source provenance;
- Chinese text, pinyin, tone marks, translation, and optional audio with stable IDs;
- production task and inspectable response dimensions;
- feedback rationale, revision prompt, and transfer task;
- accessible transcript and non-audio fallback;
- content license and third-party notices.

Do not include personal learner records, unlicensed media, or unsupported proficiency claims.
